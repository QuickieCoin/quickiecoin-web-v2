How to buy bitcoin here

  1. Open a bitcoin wallet on your phone and tap Receive. No wallet yet? Get one here before you go.
  2. At the machine, tap Buy Bitcoin and hold your wallet’s QR code up to the scanner.
  3. Verify with your phone number, feed in your cash, and confirm. The bitcoin goes straight to your wallet.

Bigger purchases need more verification, and the machine tells you on screen what it needs before you put any cash in. Bring a government‑issued photo ID if you plan to buy a lot. The limit is $25,000 per day, per customer. Buying more than that? Ask about buying by bank wire.
