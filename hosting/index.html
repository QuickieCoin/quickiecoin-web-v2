Host a bitcoin ATM in your New Jersey store

We put the machine in, we look after it, and it costs you nothing. You get paid rent for about a square foot of floor space.

QuickieCoin is a FinCEN‑registered money services business that has run bitcoin ATMs in Hudson County since 2018. Our machines sit in delis, smoke shops, wine stores and phone shops across Jersey City, Bayonne, Harrison and North Bergen. If you own a shop in or near Hudson County and you have a spare square foot near the counter, we would like to talk to you.

What do I need to host a bitcoin ATM?

Three things, and that is the whole list:

  • About one square foot of floor space. If you do not have floor space, we can mount a smaller buy‑only unit on a counter.
  • A standard power outlet. Ordinary wall current — nothing special needs installing.
  • An internet connection. Your existing shop router is fine.

What does it cost me?

Nothing. There is no purchase, no lease and no revenue share against your own takings.

QuickieCoin owns the machine and does all the work on it. We install it, service it, empty and replenish the cash, keep it stocked with bitcoin, and handle every customer question about it. Our phone number and email are on the machine and on this website, so support calls come to us, not to your staff.

What do I get?

We pay you rent for the space. What that comes to depends on the store — where you are, your hours, how the spot works for a machine — so it is something we agree with you directly rather than a number off a page. Email us and we will talk it through.

Machines are staffed entirely by us, so the rent is not tied to you doing anything: no training, no reconciliation, no handling of anyone's cash but your own.

What happens next?

  1. Email team@quickiecoin.com — or call 201‑419‑5933 — and tell us where your store is and roughly what hours you keep.
  2. We visit. One of the two owners comes to look at the spot, check the power and the internet, and answer whatever you want to ask.
  3. We install. Placement takes a morning. After that you carry on running your shop.

Common questions from store owners

Do I have to handle the cash?

No. We collect and replenish it ourselves on our own schedule. You never count it, bank it or account for it.

What if a customer has a problem with the machine?

They contact us. Our number and email are displayed on the machine. If someone does ask you, pointing them at the phone number is the whole of your involvement.

Do I need a licence to have one in my store?

No. QuickieCoin is the registered money services business and carries the regulatory obligations that go with operating the machine. You are providing floor space.

How long is the commitment?

We will go through the terms with you at the site visit rather than surprise you with them afterwards. If a machine is not working out in your store, we would rather move it than leave it somewhere it is not wanted.
