Before you come in

No wallet yet? Start with one of these.

What do I need to bring?

A bitcoin wallet app on your phone that can show its receive address as a QR code, and enough charge to open it at the machine.

What ID will it ask for?

You start with a phone number. Checks grow with the size of the buy, and the screen tells you what it needs before you put any cash in. For a larger purchase, bring a government photo ID.

How much can I buy?

Up to $25,000 a day per customer.

What does it cost?

Each machine publishes its live rate and fees on Coin ATM Radar. Open a store above and follow “Live rate and fees” for the number at that machine right now.

What happens to my information?

QuickieCoin is a FinCEN‑registered money services business, so federal law requires us to keep some customer records and report some transactions. We never sell your information or use it for marketing.

Something went wrong. Now what?

Call 201‑419‑5933 or email team@quickiecoin.com with the machine and roughly the time, and we will pull the transaction. A confirmed bitcoin send cannot be reversed; a machine that took cash and did not send, we can fix.

Buying five figures? Wire it.

Wire dollars from your bank and we send bitcoin to your wallet at a rate agreed up front, the moment the funds land.
