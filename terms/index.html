Bitcoin transactions cannot be reversed

Once a transaction is confirmed on the Bitcoin blockchain, it is final. It cannot be cancelled, recalled or refunded by us, by you, or by anyone else — there is no chargeback and no undo.

This matters most in one situation: if you provide the wrong receiving address, the bitcoin goes to that address and we cannot get it back. Check the address on your screen before you confirm. Once the transaction is confirmed we are no longer able to provide support for it.
